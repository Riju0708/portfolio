CivicPriority AI

A civic complaint portal I built for a hackathon. Citizens report a problem with photo proof and a location. AI sorts it into the right domain and ranks it by severity, and neighbors can upvote to push urgent issues up the queue.

Authorized municipal officers get a separate login to review issues and mark them solved, which moves them into a public archive.

  • Roads & Infrastructure
  • Water & Drainage
  • Power & Grid
  • Waste & Sanitation
  • Public Safety

What it does

Photo evidence and location on every report.

AI severity ranking plus community votes.

Officer-only “Mark as Solved” and a solved archive.
